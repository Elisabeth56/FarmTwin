---
title: Asking questions
description: How answers use your farm's data and the guides on your laptop.
---

Open a farm and use **Ask about this farm** below the risks. The chips above the box show what the answer takes into account: each crop and its stage, and the top risk.

- Answers appear word by word as they're written. Older laptops take longer, and the panel says what it's doing while you wait. **Stop** ends an answer early.
- Numbers in the answer link to the guide passages it used. The sources are listed under the answer.
- Recommendations become a **Do this week** checklist. Your ticks are remembered on this laptop.
- If the guides on your laptop don't cover the question, FarmTwin says so instead of guessing.

You can ask in English or Pidgin. Answers are in English.
