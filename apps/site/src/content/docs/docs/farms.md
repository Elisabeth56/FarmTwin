---
title: Farms and fields
description: How farms, fields and plantings fit together, including intercropping.
---

Each farm has fields, and each field has one or more plantings. A planting is a crop and the date it went in.

1. Click **New farm**, give it a name and the nearest town.
2. Add fields with their size in hectares.
3. Add a planting to each field. For intercropping, add two plantings to the same field.

FarmTwin covers maize, cassava, yam, sorghum, rice and cowpea. Each crop has its own colour across the app: maize is yellow, cassava tan, yam violet, sorghum rust, rice teal and cowpea blue.

Want to look around first? On the empty farm list, choose **Explore a sample farm**. Its readings are made up, and its name says so.
