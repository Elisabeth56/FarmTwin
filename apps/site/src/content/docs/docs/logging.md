---
title: Logging
description: What to log, and how stages and risks follow from it.
---

Open a farm and click **Log observation**. Log rain in millimetres, humidity, the day's highest temperature, pests, disease signs, or a free note, against the field where you saw it.

Stages and risks update as soon as you save. Logged the wrong thing? Click **Undo** in the message that appears.

## Where risks come from

Risks come from fixed rules for each crop, written from extension guides by IITA, ICRISAT and others. Click any risk to see why it fired, the readings behind it and the guide it comes from.

Dry-spell risks only appear once the farm has at least one rain reading, so a farm with nothing logged isn't treated as a dry farm.
