# Chunk: Rice planting windows, varieties and field calendar

In Northern Nigeria the guide puts rice sowing in the first two weeks of June in the Guinea savanna and the last two weeks of June in the Sudan savanna. Days to maturity depend on the variety: FARO 44 takes about 95 to 110 days, FARO 59 about 95 to 100, FARO 61 about 100 to 115, and FARO 52 about 125 to 135. Compound NPK fertilizer goes in at sowing and urea is top-dressed at about 6 to 8 weeks. Weeds are controlled at about 2 to 3 weeks and again at 5 to 6 weeks after sowing.

# Chunk: Rice blast, brown leaf spot and stem borers

Blast is a major rice disease. It is favoured by damp conditions and attacks rice both as seedlings and at flowering. In severe outbreaks losses can reach 60 to 100 percent. The guide recommends a fungicide such as Mancozeb once signs appear, so early scouting after wet spells matters. Brown leaf spot is another common leaf disease. Stem borers bore into the stem; the guide's threshold for spraying is when about 20 percent of plants are infested.

# Chunk: Rice water needs and harvest

Rice is most sensitive to water shortage at three points: tillering, panicle initiation and flowering. A dry spell at any of these stages cuts yield more than at other times. Harvest falls about 30 to 45 days after flowering. The grain should be dried to about 12 to 14 percent moisture before storage or milling.
