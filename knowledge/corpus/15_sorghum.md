# Chunk: Sorghum planting, varieties and field calendar

Sorghum varieties grown in North East Nigeria range widely in maturity, from about 75 to 180 days. For example, SAMSORG 14 matures in about 110 to 120 days and SAMSORG 17 in about 160 to 180. Planting runs from the end of May in southern Adamawa to the end of June or early July in northern Borno. Weed control is most critical in the first 6 weeks after planting. Urea top-dressing is applied before flowering.

# Chunk: Sorghum pests, diseases and storage

Shoot fly attacks young sorghum from about one week to about one month after emergence and kills the central shoot, a symptom called dead heart. The risk is higher when sowing is late. Other pests in the handbook are stalk borers, sorghum midge and head bugs; head bug feeding raises the chance of grain mould. Anthracnose can cut yield by up to 47 percent. After harvest, grain should be dried to below 12 percent moisture before storage.
